## Problem Statement

People with phone anxiety or a language barrier often avoid simple calls even when the answer matters. ChatGPT can understand the request and translate it, but without a purpose-built webpage it cannot safely structure the task, preserve consent, cross into the phone network, and return a verifiable result.

Concierge makes one promise:

> ChatGPT makes the phone call you do not want to make, while the webpage keeps you in control.

The submission wedge is one controlled Japanese hotel inquiry. The assistant gathers facts only. It cannot book, change, cancel, pay, accept a fee, or make another commitment.

## What Makes This Cool

1. The user asks ChatGPT to call a Japanese hotel about arriving after midnight.
2. ChatGPT invokes WebMCP tools. The authenticated webpage visibly becomes a complete constrained call brief.
3. The person reviews the destination, questions, disclosure, and forbidden actions.
4. The person confirms the exact current draft in the webpage.
5. A disclosed call reaches one controlled consenting destination.
6. Observed progress appears in Activity and the translated factual answer returns to ChatGPT.

WebMCP is essential rather than decorative. It lets ChatGPT prepare and inspect the webpage’s structured task without exposing the protected initiation mutation as a model tool.

## Submission Scope

The submission supports exactly:

- one server-configured destination ID;
- one allowlisted E.164 phone number;
- Japanese (`ja-JP`) for the call and English (`en`) for the result;
- one fixed hotel late-arrival objective;
- four approved question IDs;
- one call attempt, no automatic redial;
- maximum connected duration of 180 seconds;
- facts-only authority;
- no audio recording;
- structured observed events and a structured final result.

Arbitrary destinations, arbitrary languages, bookings, payments, cancellations, long-term call history, generalized transcripts, multiple call categories, and automatic retry are post-submission work.

## Constraints

- Internal freeze: September 2.
- Final proof: one real, disclosed call to a controlled consenting recipient. Simulation is local development fallback only and never appears as production Activity.
- The call-confirmation mutation is not registered through WebMCP.
- Approval is revision-bound. Every editable call field is revision-bearing; every accepted update increments the revision and invalidates approval.
- The existing Expo/mobile interface is historical reference, not product truth.
- Existing Convex safety state and the telephony bridge may be reused only after focused verification.
- The approved visual direction is a light ChatGPT-native companion surface. It must not use the OpenAI logo or imply that OpenAI approved the call.
- Pre-challenge work and challenge additions must be clearly attributable.
- The submission requires a public HTTPS URL, public repository, visible open-source license, and public video under three minutes.
- The current checkout cannot yet be described as green: the existing Vitest command produced no results for more than one minute and was stopped.
- The current dirty working tree is user-owned and must not be overwritten or mislabeled as challenge work.

## Premises

- WebMCP replaces the primary interaction surface, not authentication, durable state, policy enforcement, or telephony.
- ChatGPT is the conversation layer; the webpage has no second chatbot or composer.
- The webpage exposes task facts, decisions, observed status, and results. It does not display model reasoning.
- ChatGPT may create, revise, read, and monitor the task.
- The person initiates the call through an authenticated webpage interaction.
- The phone agent identifies itself as an AI assistant before substantive questions.

## Glossary

- **Task:** The person-owned call brief and its current revision.
- **Attempt:** The single outbound telephone execution created after confirmation.
- **Revision-bearing field:** Destination ID, objective ID, question IDs, disclosure ID, language, authority, or forbidden-action set. Any accepted change increments `revision`.
- **Observed event:** A server-accepted fact emitted by the telephony worker for a real attempt. Production Activity renders only observed events.
- **Public Activity event:** An observed event safe to show to the person and return through WebMCP. It never includes hidden reasoning, credentials, or raw provider payloads.
- **Task Activity event:** A server-committed fact about draft preparation or a protected webpage action. It is distinct from a telephony observation and names `callbridge_server` as its source.
- **Current revision:** The task revision returned by the server at the time of an operation.
- **Confirmation intent:** A short-lived server record that binds the authenticated person, task ID, expected revision, and idempotency key before dispatch.
- **Target browser:** The supported ChatGPT browser environment in which `document.modelContext.registerTool` is feature-detected and proven by the deployment spike.
- **Controlled destination:** The consenting allowlisted recipient configured by destination ID on the server; the model never supplies its raw phone number.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: New WebMCP surface over verified safety and telephony boundaries

Build a standard web app for the hotel inquiry. Register a small WebMCP tool surface, use authenticated durable task state, and reuse backend boundaries only after focused tests and one vertical call slice.

Chosen because it creates a genuinely WebMCP-native product without reimplementing the highest-risk controls before the deadline.

### Approach B: Full WebMCP-era rewrite

Conceptually cleaner, but it repeats consent, authority, dispatch, callback, and result work. It is excluded from the submission execution path and retained only in the post-submission roadmap.

The Expo Web retrofit was rejected because it preserves the wrong mobile interaction model.

## Recommended Approach

### 1. Browser surface

Create a new `web/` React/TypeScript application with one authenticated task route.

Approved review layout:

- 58px minimal Concierge header;
- centered reading column, maximum 760px;
- 336px Activity inspector at desktop widths;
- structured call object with destination, objective, questions, authority, and disclosure;
- one black **Confirm call** action and a separate **Edit brief** action;
- no dashboard, model picker, mobile tabs, second chat, gradients, or tool-name theater.

Durable visual reference: `docs/designs/assets/callbridge-review-approved.png`.

Responsive and accessibility requirements:

- At 1024px and above, show the reading column and Activity inspector side by side.
- Below 1024px, move Activity below the task; never compress both columns into slivers.
- At 640px and below, collapse two-column field rows to one column and place confirmation copy above the buttons.
- All actions are keyboard reachable with visible focus.
- Confirmation status is conveyed by text and icon, never color alone.
- Text and controls meet WCAG AA contrast.
- Support zoom to 200%, reduced motion, and screen-reader labels.
- **Confirm call** remains disabled until the current server revision, ownership, policy state, and disclosure are loaded successfully.

### 2. Target-environment spike comes first

Before building the full page, deploy a minimal secure page that proves:

1. the target ChatGPT browser exposes `document.modelContext.registerTool`;
2. a read-only tool can be registered and invoked;
3. the invocation can read authenticated server state;
4. a page state update is visible after invocation;
5. cookies, redirects, and session restoration work inside that browser.

If WebMCP is unavailable, show: “WebMCP is not available in this browser. Open this task in the supported ChatGPT browser.” Do not silently simulate tool success.

### 3. Authentication and ownership

Use the existing WorkOS deployment as the default web identity provider.

- Provision one dedicated judge-demo account and one founder account.
- Use AuthKit React with `ConvexProviderWithAuthKit`; production `devMode` is disabled and Convex validates the WorkOS JWT on every protected operation.
- Redirect unauthenticated users to WorkOS and return them to the requested task.
- Validate session and task ownership on every query and mutation.
- Keep confirmation and stopping as authenticated Convex mutations invoked only by the webpage; never register either operation through WebMCP.
- Expired sessions return `AUTH_REQUIRED` without changing task or attempt state.
- The public demo account may access only seeded demo tasks and the controlled destination.

  };
  allowedQuestionIds: [
    "after-midnight-allowed",
    "latest-check-in-time",
    "advance-notice-required",
    "late-arrival-fee"
  ];
  authority: "gather_facts_only";
  forbiddenActions: [
    "book",
    "change_reservation",
    "cancel",
    "pay",
    "accept_fee",
    "accept_terms",

Phone numbers are stored and normalized server-side. The server injects the fixed destination, objective, languages, disclosure, authority, and forbidden actions at creation; WebMCP never supplies those values. The exact Japanese disclosure must be approved by the recipient and legal reviewer, must truthfully state AI identity, speech transcription, no audio recording, and 24-hour structured retention, and must be committed into the versioned policy before external effects can be enabled. If the recipient asks for a prohibited commitment, the phone agent says it is authorized only to gather information, records a `prohibited_request_declined` event, and ends or returns to the approved questions.

### 5. Task and attempt model

Task state and telephone attempt state are separate.

```ts
type TaskStatus =
  | "draft"
  | "awaiting_confirmation"
  | "confirmed"
  | "in_progress"
  | "completed"
  | "failed"
  | "stopped";

type AttemptStatus =
  | "queued"
  | "dialing"
  | "connected"
  | "ending"
  | "ended"
  | "failed"
  | "cancelled"
  | "timed_out";

type CallDraft = {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  taskId: string;
  revision: number;
  status: TaskStatus;
  policyVersion: "hotel-ja-v1";
  owner: { isCurrentUser: true };
  destination: {
    id: "controlled-hotel";
    displayName: string;
    maskedPhone: string;
  };
  objectiveId: "late-check-in";
  questionIds: Array<DemoPolicy["allowedQuestionIds"][number]>;
  sourceLanguage: "ja-JP";
  outputLanguage: "en";
  disclosure: DemoPolicy["disclosure"];
  authority: "gather_facts_only";
  forbiddenActions: DemoPolicy["forbiddenActions"];
  confirmation: {
    state: "not_ready" | "ready" | "confirmed" | "expired";
    intentId: string | null;
    expiresAt: string | null;
  };
  createdAt: string;
  updatedAt: string;
};
```

| Current task | Current attempt | Actor/action | Next task | Next attempt | Invariant |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| none | none | WebMCP `create_call_draft` | `draft` | none | Idempotent by person + idempotency key |
| `draft` | none | WebMCP `update_call_draft` | `draft` | none | Material change: revision +1 |
| `draft` | none | webpage creates confirmation intent | `awaiting_confirmation` | none | Intent binds owner + revision and expires after 5 minutes |
| `awaiting_confirmation` | none | intent expires | `draft` | none | No dispatch; confirmation becomes `expired` |
| `awaiting_confirmation` | none | webpage confirms exact revision | `confirmed` | `queued` | One attempt; idempotent dispatch |
| `awaiting_confirmation` | none | accepted draft update | `draft` | none | Intent revoked; revision +1 |
| `confirmed` | `queued` | webpage cancels before dispatch lease | `stopped` | `cancelled` | Provider call cannot start after cancellation wins |
| `confirmed` | `queued` | worker atomically acquires dispatch lease | `in_progress` | `dialing` | Attempt ID fixed; queued cancellation now returns `INVALID_STATE` |
| `in_progress` | `dialing` | provider connects | `in_progress` | `connected` | Disclosure is the first scripted utterance |
| `in_progress` | `dialing` | no-answer timeout or provider failure | `failed` | `timed_out` or `failed` | No result fabrication |
| `in_progress` | `dialing` or `connected` | webpage requests end | `stopped` | `ending` | Hang-up requested once; prior facts may be retained |
| `stopped` | `ending` | worker confirms hang-up, or 10-second finalization deadline expires | `stopped` | `ended` | Record whether provider hang-up was observed before projecting result |
| `in_progress` | `connected` | worker completes before another terminal transition | `completed` | `ended` | Structured result required |
| `in_progress` | `connected` | remote party hangs up | `completed` | `ended` | Preserve `remote_hangup`; missing facts remain unanswered |
| `in_progress` | `connected` | 180-second deadline reached first | `failed` | `ending` | Server requests hang-up exactly once; partial observed facts may be retained |
| `failed` | `ending` | worker confirms hang-up, or 10-second finalization deadline expires | `failed` | `timed_out` | Record whether provider hang-up was observed before projecting result |
| any terminal | any terminal | duplicate or late callback | unchanged | unchanged | Event deduped and audited; terminal state never reopens |

There is no retry or future-attempt path in the submission, so there is no `stop_future_attempts` tool or UI. The webpage exposes only queued cancellation and active termination.

Page refresh or reconnect reads server state and resumes rendering. Multi-tab mutations use `expectedRevision`; the loser receives `REVISION_CONFLICT` and reloads. Only one active attempt is allowed per task.

Competing transitions use atomic compare-and-set on task and attempt state. The first accepted task-terminal transition wins. A queued cancellation loses once the dispatch lease is acquired and the UI must switch to **End call**. An end request accepted before normal completion produces `stopped`; normal completion already committed makes a later end request a no-op success returning the terminal state. The connected timeout is evaluated against the server-recorded `connectedAt`; a normal `ended` event accepted before the deadline wins, otherwise timeout wins. Result projection waits until the attempt is terminal (`ended`, `failed`, `cancelled`, or `timed_out`) or the 10-second finalization deadline forces the terminal attempt state. Late events may add private audit evidence but cannot change the public terminal outcome.

### 6. WebMCP contracts

All contracts use schema version `1`. Creation is explicitly exempt from `expectedRevision`; all later state-changing tools require it.

Stable error codes:

`AUTH_REQUIRED`, `FORBIDDEN`, `NOT_FOUND`, `INVALID_INPUT`, `REVISION_CONFLICT`, `INVALID_STATE`, `DEMO_POLICY_DENIED`, `UNSUPPORTED_ENVIRONMENT`, `INTERNAL_ERROR`.

All five tool handlers pass backend failures through one exhaustive `toWebMcpError` boundary. Existing Convex and domain codes remain internal; unknown failures become `INTERNAL_ERROR` and never expose raw provider or stack details.

| Tool | Input | Output | Idempotency and validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `create_call_draft` | `{schemaVersion:1,idempotencyKey,questionIds:[...]}` | `{taskId,revision:1,status:"draft",draft:CallDraft}` | Server injects all fixed policy fields; 1–4 distinct allowlisted questions; repeated key returns the same task |
| `update_call_draft` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId,expectedRevision,patch}` | `{taskId,revision,status:"draft",confirmationReset,draft:CallDraft}` | Allowed only in task `draft` or `awaiting_confirmation` with no attempt; only material changes increment revision |
| `read_call_draft` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId}` | `{taskId,revision,status,draft}` | Read-only; ownership required |
| `get_call_status` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId,afterActivitySequence?}` | `{taskStatus,attemptStatus?,events,nextActivitySequence}` | Public projection items only; ordered and paginated by server-owned `activitySequence` |
| `get_call_result` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId}` | `{status:"not_ready"|"processing"|"ready"|"failed",retryAfterMs?,result?,failure?}` | Nonterminal is `not_ready`; terminal projection may be `processing`; only `ready` includes `CallResult`; exhausted projection returns stable failure |

`update_call_draft.patch` may contain only `questionIds`: 1–4 distinct IDs from the policy, in the order they should be asked. It is allowed only when task status is `draft` or `awaiting_confirmation` and no attempt exists. A material change increments revision and revokes the confirmation intent; an identical patch returns the existing draft with `confirmationReset:false` and does not increment revision.

### Protected webpage mutation contracts

These authenticated Convex mutations are ownership-checked and never registered as WebMCP tools. AuthKit supplies the JWT through `ConvexProviderWithAuthKit`; there is no parallel REST or Pages Functions mutation layer.

| Operation | Input | Success | Idempotency and errors |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `hotelDemo:createConfirmationIntent` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId,expectedRevision,idempotencyKey}` | `{intentId,taskStatus:"awaiting_confirmation",revision,expiresAt}` | Allowed from `draft`; repeated key returns the same unexpired intent. Errors: stable common codes plus `INTENT_ALREADY_CONFIRMED` |
| `hotelDemo:confirmAndQueue` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId,expectedRevision,confirmationIntentId,idempotencyKey}` | `{taskStatus:"confirmed",attemptId,attemptStatus:"queued",revision}` | In one mutation, consumes the exact-revision intent, records confirmation, creates the only attempt, appends Activity, and schedules dispatch. Repeated key returns the same attempt. Errors add `INTENT_EXPIRED` |
| `hotelDemo:requestStop` | `{schemaVersion:1,taskId,attemptId,expectedRevision,idempotencyKey}` | `{taskStatus,attemptStatus}` | Cancels a reserved attempt atomically or schedules one authenticated worker hang-up for the stored Call SID. Repeated requests return current state; completed terminal state is a no-op success |

The review page creates or refreshes a confirmation intent in the background only after the exact revision and policy are rendered. The single visible **Confirm call** click consumes that already-visible-revision intent. If the intent expires or the revision changes, the button disables, the page refreshes the intent, and the person must click again; a click is never replayed automatically.

### 7. Confirmation security claim

The defensible guarantee is:

> Call initiation is not model-tool-invocable through Concierge’s WebMCP contract and requires an authenticated confirmation interaction in the webpage.

A normal browser click is not cryptographic proof of a physical human. Do not claim otherwise. The webpage may require `event.isTrusted` and active user activation before requesting a confirmation intent, but server security still rests on authentication, ownership, exact revision, short intent expiry, policy validation, idempotency, and one-attempt enforcement.

If reliable WebAuthn user verification fits the schedule, it may strengthen confirmation. It is not required for the hackathon path.

### 8. Telephony event and callback contract

The telephony worker emits signed callbacks with:

```ts
type AttemptEventBase = {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  eventId: string;
  taskId: string;
  attemptId: string;
  workerSequence: number;
  observedAt: string;
  source: "telephony_worker";
};

type AttemptEvent = AttemptEventBase & (
  | { type: "dispatch_accepted" | "dialing" | "connected"; publicPayload: {} }
  | { type: "disclosure_delivered"; publicPayload: { disclosureId: "ai-assistant-ja-v2" } }
  | { type: "question_started"; publicPayload: { questionId: DemoPolicy["allowedQuestionIds"][number] } }
  | { type: "fact_observed"; publicPayload: { questionId: DemoPolicy["allowedQuestionIds"][number]; sourceText: string; translatedValue: string; extractionConfidence: number; translationConfidence: number } }
  | { type: "prohibited_request_declined"; publicPayload: { action: DemoPolicy["forbiddenActions"][number] } }
  | { type: "policy_violation_detected"; publicPayload: { category: "unauthorized_commitment" | "forbidden_action_attempt" | "disclosure_failure"; evidenceExcerpt: string } }
  | { type: "hangup_requested"; publicPayload: { reason: "user" | "connected_timeout" | "policy" } }
  | { type: "ended"; publicPayload: { reason: "completed" | "user" | "connected_timeout" | "remote_hangup" } }
  | { type: "failed"; publicPayload: { stage: "dispatch" | "dialing" | "connection" | "callback"; code: string } }
);

type TaskActivityEvent = {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  eventId: string;
  taskId: string;
  type: "draft_created" | "draft_updated" | "confirmation_ready" | "confirmation_expired" | "confirmed" | "queued_cancelled" | "end_requested" | "result_ready";
  occurredAt: string;
  source: "callbridge_server";
  publicPayload: { revision: number };
};

type PublicActivityItem = {
  activitySequence: number;
  projectedAt: string;
  gapBefore: boolean;
  event: TaskActivityEvent | AttemptEvent;
};
```

- Authenticate callbacks with the existing shared signature scheme and timestamp tolerance.
- Deduplicate by `eventId`; enforce unique `(attemptId, workerSequence)`.
- Accept out-of-order worker delivery into the private audit log. The public projector assigns one task-scoped, monotonically increasing `activitySequence` to both task and attempt events in the transaction that makes each item public; clients paginate only on that value, so a delayed worker event cannot be skipped by an advanced cursor.
- `get_call_status` reads strictly after `afterActivitySequence` through the task-sequence index, returns at most 25 items, and never uses an unbounded collection. A demo attempt may project at most 64 public attempt events; overflow enters the private rejection log and cannot enlarge the public task.
- Buffer a worker-sequence gap for up to 2 seconds. After that, project the available event with `gapBefore:true`; a later backfill receives a new `activitySequence`. After terminal attempt state, result projection waits up to 5 seconds for missing worker sequences, records unresolved gaps as ambiguity, and then finalizes. Events arriving after a ready result remain private audit evidence and do not rewrite the result.
- A late event cannot move a terminal task back to a nonterminal state.
- Raw provider payloads remain server-side and are never returned through WebMCP.
- The worker owns call audio handling and source-language text events. Audio recording is disabled. Completed turns are persisted incrementally in the call Durable Object with limits of 2 KiB per turn, 128 turns, and 64 KiB total. Reaching a limit records `TRANSCRIPT_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`, ends safely, and permits only supportable partial facts.
- The worker runs strict structured extraction and emits signed candidate fact, disclosure, and safety events. Convex validates and deduplicates those events, then deterministically projects the authoritative English `CallResult` only from accepted evidence. It does not invent answers for unanswered questions. `commitmentSafety` becomes `possible_violation` only when at least one accepted `policy_violation_detected` event exists; `policyViolations` is the deterministic redacted projection of those events.
- Validate every discriminated payload server-side. An event body is at most 4 KB; `sourceText`, codes, and display strings are at most 240 UTF-8 characters each.
- Redact raw phone numbers, names not present in the server-owned destination display, credentials, provider IDs, and free-form model reasoning before public projection. Invalid or oversized payloads enter a private rejection log and never appear in Activity.
- Production Activity merges `TaskActivityEvent` and accepted public `AttemptEvent` records while labeling their sources. Only the latter are telephony observations.
- Raw Durable Object turns are deleted immediately after extraction. Demo task drafts, public events, private callback payloads, and results are hard-deleted after 24 hours. Cleanup state is durable and retries idempotently; overdue deletion appears in system readiness and blocks new demo calls until retention is healthy. The demo account cannot extend retention.

### 9. Result contract

```ts
type CallResult = {
  schemaVersion: 1;
  taskId: string;
  attemptId: string;
  outcome: "answered" | "partial" | "no_answer" | "failed" | "stopped";
  sourceLanguage: "ja-JP";
  outputLanguage: "en";
  summary: string | null;
  facts: Array<{
    questionId: string;
    status: "reported" | "not_answered" | "ambiguous";
    value: string | null;
    evidence: { sourceEventId: string; sourceExcerpt: string } | null;
  }>;
  durationSeconds: number;
  disclosureStatus: "delivered" | "not_observed" | "failed";
  commitmentSafety: "none_observed" | "possible_violation";
  policyViolations: Array<{ eventId: string; description: string }>;
  terminalReason: "completed" | "remote_hangup" | "no_answer" | "provider_failure" | "user_cancelled" | "user_ended" | "connected_timeout";
  terminalAt: string;
};
```

`reported` requires one accepted `fact_observed` event for the same approved question, a nonempty source excerpt, extraction confidence at or above 0.85, translation confidence at or above 0.85, and a nonempty English `translatedValue`. The result preserves at most 240 source-language characters as bounded evidence. Lower confidence, disagreement between events, or translation uncertainty becomes `ambiguous`; no accepted event becomes `not_answered`. The summary is a deterministic bounded projection of accepted fact values, not a second ungrounded model pass. The UI never calls these facts independently verified or hotel-confirmed.

Terminal mapping is exact:

| Task / attempt terminal state | Result outcome | Terminal reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `completed` / `ended` with all questions `reported` | `answered` | `completed` |
| `completed` / `ended` with any ambiguous or unanswered fact | `partial` | `completed` |
| `completed` / `ended` with reason `remote_hangup` | `answered` if all facts are reported, otherwise `partial` | `remote_hangup` |
| `failed` / dialing `timed_out` with no connection | `no_answer` | `no_answer` |
| `failed` / `failed` | `failed` | `provider_failure` |
| `stopped` / queued `cancelled` | `stopped` | `user_cancelled` |
| `stopped` / active `ended` | `stopped` | `user_ended` |
| `failed` / connected `timed_out` | `partial` if any fact was observed, otherwise `failed` | `connected_timeout` |
| any terminal task / terminal attempt after projector exhausts 3 tries | no `CallResult`; `get_call_result.status="failed"` | failure `{stage:"result_processing",code:"RESULT_PROJECTION_FAILED",retryable:false}` |

A terminal attempt queues Convex result projection from accepted signed evidence. The projector tries immediately, then twice more after 1 and 4 seconds. Until it finishes, `get_call_result` returns `{status:"processing",retryAfterMs:500}`; before any terminal attempt it returns `{status:"not_ready"}`. After the third failed try it persists and returns `{status:"failed",failure:{stage:"result_processing",code:"RESULT_PROJECTION_FAILED",retryable:false}}`; the UI stops polling, preserves task/attempt facts, and renders the factual result-processing failure with a support-safe retry option disabled for the demo account. The result page shows translated summary, per-question facts, bounded evidence, duration, disclosure status, terminal reason, and any possible policy violation. There are no generalized “call notes,” transcript, audio, or hard-coded claims that no commitment was made.

### 10. Visible states

- **Review:** current revision, destination, objective, questions, authority, disclosure, and server-committed Task Activity events.
- **Queued:** confirmation accepted, cancellation available, no claim that dialing began.
- **Calling:** destination, attempt state, elapsed time, disclosure state, latest started approved question, and active **End call**.
- **Result:** translated summary, per-question reported/ambiguous/unanswered facts, bounded evidence, duration, language, disclosure status, terminal reason, and policy-violation status.
- **Failure:** preserve the draft and name the factual stage: validation, dispatch, dialing, connection, callback, or result processing.
- **Simulation:** local development only with a persistent “Simulation” banner; excluded from production Activity and final demo proof.

### 11. Reuse gate and vertical slice

Do not build the full UI around unproven reuse. First prove:

1. focused task revision and ownership tests;
2. confirmation replay and stale-revision rejection;
3. signed callback authentication and deduplication;
4. one dispatch to the controlled destination with effects explicitly enabled;
5. one event from the worker projected into browser Activity;
6. one structured fact returned through `get_call_result`.

The minimum fallback adapter requires only provider milestones (`dialing`, `connected`, `ended`), the worker-owned disclosure event, and one final structured `fact_observed` event at hangup; live per-question progress is cut before adding another vendor or protocol. If the existing bridge cannot produce a signed `connected` event and one structured fact by August 29 at 18:00 Bangkok time, ship milestone-only Activity. If no contract-compatible adapter can complete one disclosed real call and structured result by August 30 at 18:00 Bangkok time, the real-call demo is a no-go: stop UI expansion, do not present simulation as proof, and submit only if the end-to-end requirement is recovered by the August 31 gate.
